## Root cause fixed by this release

Reported on pelicano: Control Center Extensions, Studio and
Gaming panels rendered completely black. Proven causes (shell#347,
runtime Loader harness pre/post fix):

- Studio and Gaming assigned `session: root.session` to
  `SplitPaneLayout`, which declares no `session` property, so the
  pane component failed to create.
- Extensions missed sibling imports (`import "extensions"` in
  `Panes.qml`, `import qs.services` in
  `CommunityRegistrySection.qml`), so its sections failed to
  resolve.
- `Panes.qml`'s Loader swallowed creation errors, so every
  failure rendered as a silent black rectangle. The Loader now
  logs `errorString` and shows a localized fallback.
- `tests/test_pane_load_contracts.py` locks the contract (fails
  3/4 on the pre-fix tree, passes after).

VM evidence records (`RESULT.md` files and panel screenshots
cited below) live in the maintainer QA evidence store at
`~/.local/share/hornero/install-test/evidence/<scenario>-preview31/`
on the maintainer workstation; they are not tracked in this
repository.

## Known cosmetic issue

On unencrypted installs the initramfs may print `Failed to open
encryption mapping ... is not a LUKS volume` before continuing to
a normal boot. Boot, login and desktop are unaffected.
