## Known cosmetic issue

On unencrypted installs the initramfs prints `Failed to open
encryption mapping ... is not a LUKS volume` before continuing to a
normal boot (encrypt hook present without a cryptdevice). Boot,
login and desktop are unaffected; tracked as a cosmetic follow-up.
